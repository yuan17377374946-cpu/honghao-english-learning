信息匹配 · 俱乐部

难度 ★★★ · 建议用时 6 分钟

下面 A～F 是六个俱乐部的简介。根据五位同学的运动偏好选择合适的俱乐部。

A. Guangdong Southern Tigers
The club was set up in 1993. The average (平均) age of the members is 23 years old. The club has trained Yi Jianlian and other main players of the Chinese national men's basketball team.
B. UCL Swimming Club
This is an exciting club that provides students with the chance to train and compete. The club is one of the largest sports clubs in the city.
C. Lonely Goat Running Club
The club was started about two years ago. Running can help people become fitter and healthier. The club runners always meet at weekends and help each other along the paths.
D. Youth Basketball Club
The club welcomes 7th and 8th graders. Teenagers are put into teams generally based on age. It provides the best training in the city.
E. Xuperman Sports
The club was founded by the famous Chinese ping-pong player, Xu Xin. It is to encourage more teenagers to play ping-pong. The coaches are experienced and patient.
F. Dance Sport Club
The club is for dancers of all styles, skill levels and backgrounds. It includes many dance styles, including hip hop, jazz and breaking. It offers different dance classes for children.

选择对应俱乐部 A～F

语法填空 · 学会改变思维方式

记叙文 · 难度 ★★★ · 建议用时 6 分钟

阅读短文，在空格中填入一个适当的词，或用括号内所给词的正确形式填空。

What should we do when we meet problems? Maybe changing the way we think will bring (we) a new idea.

My daughter had a blue blouse (衬衫). She thought that it was one of the best (present) she had ever had. She loved to wear it anytime except on schooldays, she had to wear uniforms at school. One day, she found the blouse became smaller. Feeling upset, she (cry) sadly.

At the suggestion of my friend, I took my daughter to the tailor's. The master looked the blouse and said, “If you trust me, I will make the blouse longer.” He explained that he would make the blouse another style. We couldn't (total) understand him, but we decided to leave the blouse there.

Two days later, I went to get the blouse back. To my surprise, I found that the blouse (turn) into a dress. The master cut off some parts of the blouse and added other parts. The blouse took on a new style and looked (beautiful) than before. My daughter couldn't wait to try it on when she saw it. She smiled happily.

Life is not always smooth. Actually, we can hardly avoid (meet) problems. Therefore, we are supposed to change ourselves and think about things in a different way. Change isn't bad thing; instead, it is a valuable way of thinking. All in all, only through change can progress be made.
