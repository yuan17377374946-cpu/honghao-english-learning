阅读理解 · A 篇

深中龙岗学校九上期中英语 · 第 11–15 题

点英文单词查中文意思，点选项圆圈作答。

第 1 段On July 15, 2026, a deliveryman（外卖员）named Wang Jibing won the Lu Xun Literature Prize, one of the top literary honors in China, for his third poetry collection Flying Low.

第 2 段Writing seemed to be the only light in his dark life. Born in 1969 in Xuzhou, he lived an unhappy childhood. Later, he dropped out of middle school and took whatever jobs he could find. However busy he was, he kept writing in his spare time. In 1992, Wang returned to his hometown to make a living. Unluckily, his father was worried that writing could never support a family, so he burned all his manuscripts（手稿）, including a 200,000-character novel.

第 3 段That was not the end and Wang never put down his pen. He then turned from writing novels to poetry. In 2018, he became a food deliveryman to support his family. In order to gather materials for his writing, he interviewed over 140 delivery workers. Based on the first-hand information, he wrote poems to describe ordinary deliverymen who live a hard life yet remain filled with hope.

第 4 段Although some people think he is too positive, he continues spreading warmth instead of showing only darkness. "Not all wings can soar（翱翔）high, but flying low is still flying," he says—a belief that helps him discover the meaning of being ordinary.

第 11–13 题先找原文依据。第 14–15 题再练观点推断和选标题。

11. What do we know about Wang Jibing from Paragraph 1?
第 11 题答案与解析

B · 细节推断

his third poetry collection

third 表明这已经是他的第三部诗集，所以他写过不止一本。Lu Xun 是奖项名称的一部分，原文没有说鲁迅是他的榜样。

12. What is Wang’s father’s attitude towards his writing?
第 12 题答案与解析

D · 态度与同义转述

his father was worried that writing could never support a family

先找 worried，再读出父亲担心的事。support a family 是「养家」，make a living by writing 是「靠写作谋生」。

13. Why did Wang interview lots of delivery workers according to Paragraph 3?
第 13 题答案与解析

C · 目的与同义替换

In order to gather materials for his writing

Why 问原因或目的，第三段的 In order to 直接引出目的。gather materials 与 collect materials 都是「收集素材」。

14. Which of the following might Wang Jibing agree with?
第 14 题答案与解析

C · 观点推断

Not all wings can soar high, but flying low is still flying.

「低飞」是比喻。结合第三段的 remain filled with hope，可以推断他认为平凡人也能带着希望继续努力。

15. Which can be the best title of the passage?
第 15 题答案与解析

D · 标题与文章主线

won the Lu Xun Literature Prize … he kept writing in his spare time

首段写获奖，后几段回顾他坚持写作、转写诗歌和从送餐经历中取材的过程。D 同时概括了人物身份和写作历程。

逐题答案与知识点

第 36 题答案与解析

a · 冠词

government official 是单数可数名词，首次泛指一位官员。government 以辅音音素开头，用 a。

第 37 题答案与解析

to follow · 固定搭配

encourage somebody to do something 表示「鼓励某人做某事」，him 后接 to follow。

第 38 题答案与解析

met · 一般过去时

这一段在回顾徐霞客过去的旅行经历。meet 的过去式是不规则形式 met。

第 39 题答案与解析

him · 人称代词宾格

troubled 是动词，空格充当它的宾语，所以用 he 的宾格 him。

第 40 题答案与解析

but · 转折连词

前面说腿疼得厉害，后面说仍然不肯放弃，两个分句是转折关系。but 是参考答案，yet 在此也能表达转折。

第 41 题答案与解析

carefully · 形容词变副词

空格修饰动词 recorded，说明记录的方式，用副词 carefully。

第 42 题答案与解析

were noted · 一般过去时的被动语态

山形、河流走向和岩石是「被记录」的，by him 提示被动。叙述过去，且主语为复数，所以用 were noted。

第 43 题答案与解析

more successful · 形容词比较级

than 提示比较级，become 后用形容词。successful 是多音节词，比较级为 more successful。

第 44 题答案与解析

explorer · 动词变名词

a great 后需要一个表示人的单数可数名词。explore 是「探索」，explorer 是「探险家」。

第 45 题答案与解析

of · 介词短语

in memory of somebody 表示「为了纪念某人」，所以填 of。
