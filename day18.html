01

模仿朗读 · 4分

Wind Power

听1遍 · 准备50秒

先只听原音，注意停顿和语调；再开始50秒准备，最后录下自己的朗读并回放。

🎧 真题原音

Do you know about wind power? People began to use the power of wind long ago. In ancient times, it was used to move large ships. Around the end of the 19th century, new technology made it possible to use wind to produce electricity. Now, there are a lot of wind farms around the world. They can even be built in the sea.

02

信息获取 · 听选信息 · 6分

三段对话

每段听2遍 · 口头回答

先看问题和候选信息，再播放音频。选定答案后，要把英文答案开口读出来。

下面的候选选项根据录音补配，用于课堂练习，并非原题屏幕截图。

Dialogue 1 · Summer Holiday

1. Which city is the man going to visit?

2. How many times has the man been there?

W: What’s your plan for this summer holiday, Mike?
M: I’m going to visit Paris to watch the Olympics.
W: Sounds great. Have you been there before?
M: Yes. I’ve been there twice.

Dialogue 2 · An Appointment

3. Who is the man?

4. When will they meet tomorrow?

W: Dr Lee’s office. Can I help you?
M: This is Jason, a patient of Dr Lee’s. Can I meet him at 9 a.m. tomorrow?
W: Let me check. He won’t be free at that time. What about 10 a.m.?
M: Okay. See you tomorrow.
W: See you.

Dialogue 3 · Buying a Dress

5. Where does the conversation probably take place?

6. What colour does the woman like best?

M: How can I help you, Madam?
W: I need a dress for a party.
M: We have many beautiful dresses. What about this green one?
W: Well, I like red best.
M: No problem. We have a red one here.
W: Great. Let me try it on.

03

信息获取 · 回答问题 · 4分

Robin and the Dog

听2遍 · 每题8秒

先根据疑问词判断要听“原因、次数、是否或评价”，播放两遍后口头回答。

🎧 真题原音

7. Why couldn’t Robin’s mother take care of the dog?

8. How often did Robin walk the dog?

9. Did Robin keep the dog clean?

10. What did Robin think of the dog?

One day, a neighbour asked Robin’s mother to take care of his dog for a week. However, she was too busy to look after it. Robin volunteered to help, and his mother agreed. He fed the dog and walked it twice a day. He always kept the dog clean, no matter how tiring it was. In Robin’s mind, the dog was a pleasant and lovely friend. He really loved it.

依次读出四个答案
04

短文复述 · 8分

Safety Education Day

听2遍 · 准备60秒 · 复述60秒

第一遍记“谁、什么时候、做了什么”，第二遍补充细节；复述时尽量覆盖下面6个要点。

🎧 真题原音
① 时间last Friday
② 嘉宾a police officer
③ 讲座protect ourselves
④ 活动a role play
⑤ 礼物some postcards
⑥ 感受happy and meaningful

I’m Peter. Our school had a Safety Education Day last Friday. A police officer was invited as the guest. He gave us a talk on how to protect ourselves from danger. After the talk, I took part in a role play. It was great fun. At the end of the day, I got some postcards as presents. I was very happy. All of us thought it was a meaningful day.

尽量使用完整、简单的句子
